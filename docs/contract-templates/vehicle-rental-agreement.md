## 5. Handover and Condition of the Vehicle

5.1 At pick-up and at return, the Owner (or their representative) and the Renter inspect the Vehicle together and record on the Platform: the odometer reading, the fuel level, the Vehicle's condition and any existing damage, supported by photographs including a photo of the dashboard showing the mileage and fuel level.

5.2 Both parties confirm each handover record by signing it on the Platform. The signed records are the agreed evidence of the Vehicle's condition, mileage and fuel level at pick-up and return.

5.3 Damage, missing items or excess wear found at return that were not recorded at pick-up are presumed to have occurred during the Rental Period, unless the Renter shows otherwise.

## 6. Fuel and Mileage

6.1 The Vehicle must be returned with at least the same fuel level recorded at pick-up. Any shortfall is charged at the current pump price plus a reasonable refuelling fee.

6.2 Unless agreed otherwise in writing on the Platform, there is no mileage limit for travel within Kenya.

## 7. Drivers

7.1 **Self-drive bookings:** only the Renter may drive the Vehicle. The Renter confirms that they hold a valid driving licence for this class of vehicle, have uploaded it to the Platform, and will carry it whenever driving.

7.2 **Chauffeured bookings:** only the Owner's appointed chauffeur may drive the Vehicle. The Renter will not ask the chauffeur to break any traffic law, work unreasonable hours, or drive outside the agreed trip.

## 8. Use of the Vehicle

The Renter must not, and must not allow anyone else to:

1. use the Vehicle for any illegal purpose, or carry illegal goods;
2. drive under the influence of alcohol, drugs or any substance that affects driving;
3. sub-let, lend or hire out the Vehicle, or use it to carry passengers or goods for payment;
4. use the Vehicle for racing, driving lessons, towing, or off-road driving, unless the Owner has agreed in writing on the Platform;
5. take the Vehicle outside Kenya without the Owner's prior written consent on the Platform;
6. carry more passengers than the Vehicle's seating capacity, or load it beyond its safe limit;
7. smoke in the Vehicle.

## 9. Accidents, Damage and Theft

9.1 If the Vehicle is involved in an accident, damaged, broken into or stolen, the Renter must immediately inform the Owner through the Platform and report the incident to the nearest police station within 24 hours, obtaining a police abstract.

9.2 The Renter must not admit liability, or agree any settlement with a third party, without the Owner's consent.

9.3 The Vehicle is covered by the Owner's comprehensive insurance. The Renter is responsible for: the insurance excess for any claim arising during the Rental Period; any loss or damage the insurer refuses to cover because of the Renter's breach of this Agreement (including clause 8); and loss or damage not covered by insurance.

9.4 The Renter is responsible for traffic offences, fines, parking and toll charges incurred during the Rental Period.

## 10. Breakdown and Maintenance

10.1 The Owner is responsible for routine maintenance and for mechanical failures not caused by the Renter's misuse.

10.2 If the Vehicle breaks down, the Renter must inform the Owner through the Platform and must not arrange repairs without the Owner's consent, except minor repairs needed for safety, which the Owner will refund against a receipt.

## 11. Ending the Agreement Early

11.1 The Owner may end this Agreement and recover the Vehicle if the Renter seriously breaches it, including any breach of clause 8.

11.2 If the Renter returns the Vehicle early, any refund is governed by the cancellation policy referred to in clause 4.3.

## 12. Communication

All communication about this booking takes place through the Platform's messaging. The parties agree not to exchange personal phone numbers or arrange payment outside the Platform.

## 13. Personal Data

Each party's personal information in this Agreement is used only to perform it and is handled in line with the Data Protection Act, 2019 and the Platform's Privacy Policy.

## 14. Governing Law and Disputes

This Agreement is governed by the laws of Kenya. The parties will first try to resolve any dispute in good faith through the Platform. Disputes that cannot be resolved that way may be referred to the courts of Kenya.
